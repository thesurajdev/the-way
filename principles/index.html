Ten Core Principles

The ten principles of The Way. Each page carries the short statement, the full explanation, a practical reading, and what it does not mean.

Part of The Way v1.0

The Ten Principles in One Line

  1. Find your own way.
  2. Keep belief personal.
  3. Respect their way.
  4. Do not force.
  5. Grow yourself.
  6. Grow together.
  7. Respect nature.
  8. Think, question, and change.
  9. Consider the context.
  10. Let The Way evolve.
1

Find Your Own Way

Every person is unique.

2

Keep Belief Personal

What you believe is your choice.

3

Respect Their Way

Other people are individuals too.

4

Do Not Force

Share your ideas.

5

Grow Yourself

Life is an opportunity to experience, learn, create, understand, connect, and grow.

6

Grow Together

Individuality does not mean that we live alone.

7

Respect Nature

We are part of nature.

8

Think, Question, and Change

Do not accept an idea merely because it is old.

9

Context Matters

Life is not identical in every place, time, culture, or circumstance.

10

Let The Way Evolve

The Way itself is not above questioning.

The Core Idea

If all ten principles had to be reduced to one:

Find your way, respect the freedom and dignity of others to find theirs, and move toward personal, human, and natural flourishing without forcing one way upon everyone.

Find your way. Respect theirs. Grow together.
