Discuss

You don’t need to agree with The Way to participate in its discussion.

Opening soon

The discussion space is organized around ideas, not agreement. Moderation addresses behavior, not ideological position — criticism of The Way is not removed for being criticism of The Way.

In this first release the site is an open publication; the discussion space is being prepared.

Discussion categories

Philosophy

What the ideas mean, how they fit together, where they are unclear.

Criticism

Contradictions, harmful implications, outdated ideas, better explanations.

Questions

Things the document does not answer well — or at all.

Alternatives

Other philosophies, and what they do better.

Experiences

How any of this works — or does not work — in a real life.

Nature

Responsibility toward the world we share.

Society

Coexistence, law, culture, and difference in practice.

Future

What should change in the next version, and why.

Moderation addresses behavior, not belief.

Do not remove criticism simply because it criticizes The Way. Do not rank people by agreement. Accounts, if added, will exist for technical reasons only — never as philosophical status.

A philosophy of freedom should be able to survive someone’s freedom to reject it.

No idea within The Way is above examination — and neither is this website.

If a discussion system is added, accounts will exist only for technical and community reasons, never as philosophical status.
