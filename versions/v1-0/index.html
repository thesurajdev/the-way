What changed

Recorded under Article XXII (changes should be documented rather than quietly rewriting history).

Principle 8 (Think, Question, and Change) standardized to one four-line formulation in all four places it appears.

Two of the four variants were missing “Do not reject something merely because it is new,” and the Manifesto used a different verb. The full four-line set is the symmetric superset.

Proposed by: Reconciliation pass, approved by the founder

Question 15 reworded so it stands on its own.

The previous wording asked why age 16 was “originally” mentioned, but most of the document no longer contains that claim. The answer still introduces sixteen as a personal preference, not a rule.

Proposed by: Reconciliation pass, approved by the founder

Spelling and typography normalized (recognize; typographic apostrophes).

Consistency across the published text.

Proposed by: Reconciliation pass

Why

Article XXII requires that changes be documented rather than quietly rewriting history; Article XXIII requires visible versions. This entry records the state of the document set at first publication.
